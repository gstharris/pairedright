How it works
From sign-up to first order in under an hour.
1
AI pulls your restaurant data
Type your restaurant name. Sous AI finds your Google listing, pulls your hours, address, and website, and pre-fills your profile. Your menu is extracted automatically from your website or an uploaded PDF.
~10 minutes
2
Configure tables, staff, and hardware
Add your floor plan, seat staff with PINs, and connect any printers. No technician, no site visit. If you have a receipt printer, we support it. If not, skip it.
~20 minutes
3
Print QR codes. Start service.
Print table QR codes from any printer. Guests scan and order. Kitchen sees tickets instantly. You see everything from your phone. That's it.
~5 minutes
